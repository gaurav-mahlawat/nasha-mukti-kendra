Extended Residential Recovery in a Green, Peaceful Setting

Our Residential Rehabilitation Centre at Hanuman Nagar, Keshupura is where long-term healing happens. It combines counselling, group therapy, NA meetings, peer education, yoga, skill training and a structured daily routine in an open, green setting.

Here, residents rebuild discipline, purpose and livelihood — because recovery is not complete at discharge. We treat livelihood as part of treatment, not something that happens afterwards.

  • ✓ Landscaped green grounds for yoga, sports and daily outdoor time
  • ✓ Air-conditioned private rooms with hot water
  • ✓ Skill training, vocational guidance & employment support
  • ✓ On-site NA meetings following the twelve-step programme
  • ✓ 24-hour CCTV monitoring & round-the-clock security

Rehab Services

Daily Programme at the Rehab Centre

The extended residential programme that rebuilds the mind, body and purpose.

🩺

Psychiatric Care

Ongoing psychiatric review through the residential stay, with treatment plans adjusted as recovery progresses.

💬

Individual Counselling

Regular one-to-one sessions covering triggers, craving management, coping strategies and relapse prevention.

👥

Group Therapy

Structured group sessions where residents share experience and build peer accountability.

🕊️

Narcotics Anonymous (NA)

On-site twelve-step NA meetings, with linkage to community meetings before discharge.

🎓

Peer Education

Sessions led by trained peer educators with lived experience of addiction and recovery.

🧘

Yoga & Meditation

Daily yoga, pranayama and meditation on the open grounds.

🏋️

Recreation & Fitness

Outdoor sports, walking and physical activity built into the daily routine.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Counselling

Sessions for family members on supporting recovery, setting healthy boundaries and recognising early warning signs.

Livelihood & Aftercare

Skill Development & Employment Support

Sustained recovery depends on structure, income and purpose. We treat livelihood as part of treatment.

01

Skill Training

Practical skill sessions during the residential programme, giving residents a usable trade to build on after discharge.

02

Vocational Guidance

One-to-one guidance on suitable work based on prior experience, education and physical capacity.

03

Employment Support

Help finding work after treatment — interview preparation and connections with employers open to hiring people in recovery.

04

Aftercare Linkage

Continued contact after discharge, combining employment follow-up with NA participation and family support.
