Our Story

Serving Jaipur Since 2018 — Healing Hearts & Homes

Taruchaya Seva Sansthan (Reg.) was founded with a simple belief — no life is beyond repair. It is a registered society (Reg. No. COOP/2018/JAIPUR/102147), operating since 2018 as one of Jaipur's trusted de-addiction centres.

The organisation runs two dedicated units — a detoxification & treatment centre at Bhankrota (Ajmer Road) and a residential rehabilitation centre at Keshupura. The same qualified clinical team serves both units, treating not just the addiction, but the person behind it.

Led by Director Shri Chandrashekhar Mishra and Secretary Shri Ashish Mishra, we serve patients from Jaipur and across Rajasthan — with families placing their trust in us during their hardest moments, and we honour that trust every single day.
