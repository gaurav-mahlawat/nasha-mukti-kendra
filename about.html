Our Story

15+ Years of Healing Hearts & Homes

Taruchaya Seva Sansthan was founded with a simple belief — no life is beyond repair. What started as a small counselling initiative has grown into one of Jaipur's most trusted Nasha Mukti Kendras, located on Ajmer Road near Bhankrota.

Over the years, more than 1,200 individuals have walked out of our doors with renewed lives. Our team of doctors, psychiatrists, counsellors and yoga therapists work together to treat not just the addiction, but the person behind it.

We serve patients from Jaipur and across Rajasthan, with families placing their trust in us during their hardest moments — and we honour that trust every single day.
