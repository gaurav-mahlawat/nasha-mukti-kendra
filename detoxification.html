Detox Unit
Stage 1 · Bhankrota Unit

Safe, Medically Supervised Withdrawal Management

Our Detox & Treatment Centre at Bhankrota, Ajmer Road is the first stage of treatment for anyone still using substances. Residents are physically stabilised under 24-hour medical and nursing cover before therapeutic work begins.

With 100+ beds, dedicated detox rooms, an on-site medical room and round-the-clock security, this unit makes the hardest first days of recovery safe, comfortable and dignified.

  • ✓ 2 dedicated detox rooms under close medical observation
  • ✓ 24x7 doctors, nursing cover & on-call medical officer
  • ✓ 17 air-conditioned resident rooms with attached washrooms & hot water
  • ✓ Psychiatric assessment on admission by qualified psychiatrists
  • ✓ 24-hour CCTV monitoring & security personnel

Treatment Services

What You Receive at the Detox Unit

Every service is delivered by our qualified medical and counselling team under one roof.

💊

Medical Detoxification

Supervised withdrawal management with round-the-clock nursing cover and medical supervision.

🧠

Psychiatric Assessment

Evaluation by a qualified psychiatrist on admission to identify co-occurring conditions and build a personal treatment plan.

💬

Individual Counselling

One-to-one sessions covering triggers, craving management, coping strategies and relapse prevention planning.

👥

Group Therapy

Structured group sessions where residents share experience and build peer accountability.

🕊️

Narcotics Anonymous (NA)

On-site NA meetings following the twelve-step programme, with linkage to community meetings after discharge.

🎓

Peer Education

Sessions led by trained peer educators with lived experience of addiction and recovery.

🧘

Yoga & Meditation

Daily yoga, pranayama and meditation supporting sleep, anxiety management and physical recovery.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Counselling

Sessions for family members on supporting recovery, setting healthy boundaries and recognising early warning signs.
